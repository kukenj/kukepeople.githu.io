Fast Facts

Kuke (ght) is part of the Sino-Tibetan family and Sino-Tibetan, Tibeto-Burman, Western Tibeto-Burman, Bodish, West Bodish, Ghale class.
Population
There are approximately 2,000 Kutang Bhotia individuals living in Nepal.
Language
They speak Kuke (also classified under the Sino-Tibetan language family), which reflects their distinct trans-Himalayan cultural heritage.
Religion
Their primary religion is Tibetan Buddhism.

Making Alcohol

kom arsang nang ne balu put.
Inside the alcohol-making room, we put in the balu [ingredient].
ani mo po ghar
Then [we use] the large pot.
po jhen gi aning nang ne qui put
Inside the pot, we put the [qui] mixture.
que jhen gi chine
We mix it well.
ra re put ra dek ei put ra dek
We put it in and check it carefully.
po ghar jhen gi batta gar
The large pot is placed on the fire.
batta jhen gi, then shulok, chedak sum chedak she put dhu, then jhen ho.
When it is on the fire, after three or four days, it becomes ready.

Story of Guru Rinpoche

guru rinpocho rung be ga na?
Where did Guru Rinpoche come from?
nag khushi dhongpo tak yang be
He came to make the black people happy.
hung shipu hei ge menthok ei per hei hung, ghumpu hei la menthok per hei ne
Flowers bloomed on the trees, and flowers bloomed on the bushes.
dhampo guru rinpoche
First, [there was] Guru Rinpoche.
mahure de cha nag bhu nepu shur hei hung
He came from the land of Mahure, from the direction of Nepal.
guru rinpoche shur re ba gha, guru rinpoche qhe re ba gha
Guru Rinpoche came from there; Guru Rinpoche came from that place.
kha hung sae wak ne ship hei ne
He taught people through his speech.

Story of Tooth

nei chun bhangal, wah bhangal, wah dhukpa khal
My child’s teeth broke; the teeth were broken and painful.
nag bhunga khel gham
The black cavity became large.
na thet, na phang re
It came out, and it was exposed.
shae hung ha, ra re dhup re shae hung ah
It hurt; it hurt again and again.
ani shae yang phok dhu, shae yang phok dhu
Then the tooth came out; the tooth came out.
ba ei khu ah, ung sa ba yang, mam mam kha
It was swollen, and the whole area was swollen.
